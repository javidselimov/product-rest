The Central Bank's communication is organised to inform all target groups about its mission, strategic and operational objectives, and performance results. Prompt, comprehensive, clear and accurate disclosure of information to the public supports a better understanding of the policy pursued by the Central Bank and thereby helps to shape economic and monetary expectations.

The Central Bank communicates transparently. Within this framework, regular events, conferences, briefings and various sessions are held with the participation of the media. The Central Bank also actively responds to media inquiries.
